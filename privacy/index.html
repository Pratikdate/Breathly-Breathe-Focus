Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Welcome to Breathly. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information.

1. Complete Data Privacy

We do not collect any personal data. Your health data, session history, and preferences are stored entirely locally on your device.

  • No Login Required: You never have to create an account.
  • On-Device Storage: All session data stays on your phone.
  • 100% Free: No in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no tracking.

2. Information We Collect

The Breathly app collects absolutely zero Personal Identifiable Information (PII). We may use standard, anonymous, opt-in crash reporting tools natively provided by the OS to help us fix bugs, but we do not track your usage or collect analytics.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data. The app operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.

4. Data Retention

Because all data is stored strictly locally on your device, your data is retained for exactly as long as you keep the Breathly app installed. We do not store, copy, or back up your data on any remote servers. Your data is immediately and permanently deleted the moment you uninstall the app or manually clear the app's data in your device settings.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

Data Deletion Policy

Because we do not store your data on our servers, you have full control over it. To delete all your data, simply uninstall the Breathly app from your device, or clear the app data in your device settings. This will permanently erase all your stored sessions and preferences.
