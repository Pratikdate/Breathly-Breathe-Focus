Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Welcome to BreatheApp. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. The BreatheApp collects minimal information to function effectively:

  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous data on how you interact with the app (e.g., session durations, features used) to improve our services.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic device information such as OS version for compatibility and bug fixing.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, phone numbers, or exact location data unless explicitly provided by you for support purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the application.
  • Analyze app usage and optimize user experience.
  • Address technical issues and provide customer support.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (such as analytics tools) that may collect information used to identify you. These services have their own privacy policies governing their data collection practices.

4. Security of Your Data

We value your trust in providing us your information, and we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
